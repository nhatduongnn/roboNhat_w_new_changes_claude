What a concentration is, and what tuning did to it

Every factor's prior is a weight the model calls a concentration. It is computed from the motif alone, before any data is seen, so two factors at the same λ sit orders of magnitude apart. This sheet shows where each starting concentration comes from and what λ made of it. Two tuning campaigns have now finished: the counts converged on MacIsaac, the sites did not. Rossi's ChIP-exo peaks, held out throughout, rank the rounds the same way, and show that pushing counts onto MacIsaac discarded sites Rossi supports.

The concentration is a property of the motif

RoboCOP's HMM leaves a hub state at every position and enters one factor's block. The probability of entering factor k is built in three steps, all before the model sees any Fiber-seq data.

STEP 1

Score the motif's own best word

Kd = Πi bg(b*i) / pi(b*i)

For each column i, take the base the motif most prefers, b*, and divide the genome's background frequency of that base by the motif's own probability for it. Multiply across columns. parameterize.py:37

STEP 2

Three weights are set by hand

wempty=1 · wnuc=35 · wunknown=0.1

Unbound DNA anchors the scale at 1 and the nucleosome is fixed at 35. The 10 bp unknown state, a flat matrix with no sequence preference, is given 0.1. Every other factor's weight is its Kd.

STEP 3

Weights become probabilities

αk = wk · α0Lk,  Σk wk α0Lk = 1

One unbound probability α0 is solved for as the root of that polynomial, then each weight is scaled by it raised to the factor's footprint length. Longer footprints pay more. concentration_probability_conversion.py:12

To initialize the probabilities, we assign weight 1 to the "empty" DBF (representing an unbound DNA nucleotide) and 35 to the nucleosome. To each TF, we assign a weight which is that TF's dissociation constant KD. RoboCOP, Methods

Why a sharp motif starts out rare

Kd falls geometrically with motif length and information content. A long, decisive motif scores near 1 for its favourite base in every column, so each column contributes roughly the background frequency, about a quarter. A short, vague motif barely differs from background, so each column contributes close to 1.

The cancellation worth knowing. At its own consensus a motif's sequence likelihood beats background by exactly 1/Kd — the same quantity, inverted. Setting the weight to Kd cancels that, so every factor is about equally likely to be called at its own best word. The starting concentration therefore encodes how sharp a motif is, not how abundant the protein is. Nothing in it was measured from data: robocop_em.py pins iterations = 0, so the Baum-Welch training the paper describes never runs in this fork, and λ is the only thing that moves these numbers. When Baum-Welch was forced on, it moved them the wrong way; see below.

What eight rounds of λ bought

Both campaigns are finished. Each decoded the genome, counted every factor's calls, moved its λ toward the MacIsaac count, and repeated — eight rounds, λunknown pinned at 0.01 and the nucleosome prior held throughout.

The counts converged; the site accuracy did not follow. On this evidence λ sets how many calls a factor makes, not which ones are right — a count knob, not an accuracy knob. Better site accuracy has to come from discrimination: the Fiber-seq emission model and the PWMs, not another round of λ. Rossi's held-out peaks, checked below, agree.

Factors that ran out of λ

λ is clamped to [1e-6, 1e3]. A factor at the cap still calls too few sites and the loop flags it can't fix. A factor at the floor still calls too many and nothing flags it — tune_concentrations.py's can't-fix rule only watches the cap, so these over-callers end the campaign silently pinned.     What that concentration produced

    Copies decoded genome-wide against the tuned concentration, both on log scales. The response is sublinear: a hundredfold change in concentration moves the count by roughly threefold, which is why the tuning loop divides its steps by that elasticity.

    Every factor, every number

    Precision is the share of a factor's calls (posterior ≥ 0.10) that fall within 30 bp of a MacIsaac site; recall is the share of its MacIsaac sites found.
