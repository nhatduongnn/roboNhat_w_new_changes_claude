How to read this

Enrichment is the statistic to trust. It is the mean posterior at reference sites divided by the mean everywhere else, so it does not depend on where the call threshold is drawn. Precision and recall both do — they use 0.30 × the factor's chromosome-wide maximum, floored at 0.10, which is the same threshold score_robocop.py uses for ABF1.

A false positive here is not necessarily a false positive. The Rossi reference is ChIP-exo derived, so a site that was never ChIPped looks like a model error. Precision is therefore a comparison between runs, not an absolute score. Only 74 of the model's 153 motifs have Rossi ground truth at all.

The two references disagree by construction. Rossi and MacIsaac were built independently and do not agree on where ABF1 and REB1 sites are, or how many there are. Where a conclusion depends on which one is selected, it is not yet a conclusion.

“State carries no mass” is a result, not a gap. EM drove some priors to zero, and a state with zero posterior across the whole chromosome has a precision of 0/0 and an enrichment of 0/0. Those cells are labelled rather than drawn, because rendering 0/0 as infinite enrichment would rank an extinct state as the best detector on the page.

Low counts are noise. Factors with fewer than five reference sites on this chromosome are pooled into a single row rather than charted, because precision there moves in steps larger than the differences between runs.
