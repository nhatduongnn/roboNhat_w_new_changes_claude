RoboCOP recap

An HMM that turns sequence + accessibility into occupancy of nucleosomes and TFs; each factor has a prior weight.

SequencePWM score at each motif position
×
Accessibilityupstream: MNase-seq layers
Here: Fiber Watson + Cricklayers 5 and 6 · per A, per strand: k methylated (m6A) of n reads
emission = sequence × Fiber Watson × Fiber Crick
P(k | n, state j) = Binomial(k; n, pj)
  • Hidden states: unbound DNA · one block per TF motif (fwd and rev) · a 147 bp nucleosome
  • Emissions: sequence (PWM) × accessibility layers (upstream: MNase-seq)
  • Output: a posterior for each position and factor; a factor is called where its posterior ≥ 0.10
  • Each factor has a prior weight, its “concentration”
  • Here: + two Fiber-seq layers (Watson, Crick), a binomial m6A count per state

Why the priors need tuning

Priors are never learned, EM collapses them, and the decoder only uses w = Kd·λ per factor.

  • Starting weight = the motif's Kd, which encodes motif sharpness, not abundance. Nhp6a (7 bp) starts 2,478× above Abf1 (14 bp).
  • Upstream EM is off here (iterations = 0). Forced on for 10 iterations: 28 factors pinned at the cap, 66 driven to exactly 0, ABF1 moved 3,723× up; chrXIV ABF1 0/19 sites.
Audit finding The decoder uses only w = Kd·λ per factor. The paper's α₀L normalisation is bookkeeping. Our old tuner held the stored nucleosome prior fixed, which distorted the model, so it was rebuilt (slide 8).

Hidden · after slide 8 Ground truth and tuning

Tuning search rules

These rules only control how the search moves concentrations between rounds, not the model itself.

How a concentration may move

  • At most 10× per round, up or down
  • A group with zero calls takes a plain 10× step up
  • Per-bp weight cap w1/L ≤ 0.70: no short motif gets a concentration so high it would tile the genome
  • Within ±1.25× of target: no step; once the target is bracketed, bisect
  • Count elasticity β starts at 0.5

Held fixed

  • unknown at w = 1e-3
  • nucleosome weight 35; its copy number is monitored only

In plain terms: each factor's concentration changes at most tenfold per round, and small motifs cannot be pushed so high that they cover the genome.

Part 4 Results and next steps

Lessons and next steps

Where these runs point so far, and what to try on the fiber layer.

  • EM doesn’t seem to help.
  • Tuning to MacIsaac brings site counts closer, but with the fiber layer on, accuracy barely improves.
  • Tuning the sequence layer alone seems to give better accuracy; not really for the fiber layer.
  • The fiber layer seems to carry more weight than the sequence layer.
  • In aggregate, the fiber layer may only do so much, or we can try to fix its emission:
    • coverage weighting
    • footprint width
    • the fallback rate for the 141 un-fitted TFs
