Click a row of the window table to show that run; compare all overlays every run with one dash pattern each. The run and the ticked tracks are kept when you switch region.

What the table counts. For each run, calls are the centres of contiguous stretches with posterior ≥ 0.10 for any of the 61 tuned motifs (the tuning loop's own rule, score_robocop.call_abf1), computed on the window ±250 bp and counted by centre inside the 4 kb window. At MacIsaac / at Rossi: calls matched one-to-one to a same-group reference within 30 bp, motifs of a group pooled (rossi_validate.fast_match). Rossi scores are flattering for the 12 factors with a fitted Fiber-seq footprint (trained on Rossi peaks) in the runs with the fiber layer.

Caveats. Posteriors are stored in 0.01 steps (values under 0.005 draw as 0; the decode itself already zeroes anything under 1e-4). background is not stored. other motifs is the summed posterior of the 92 motifs outside the tuned set: zero in the masked tuner-v2 runs, live only in the legacy u001 run.
